What you can ask

The agent handles two kinds of calls from start to finish.

Get a car insurance quote

Tell it about yourself and your car. It asks Liberty Shield, Summit Mutual and Harborline at the same time and reads back the prices, cheapest first.

"Hi, I'd like a quote for full coverage on a 2019 Toyota Corolla."

Report an accident claim

It checks you're safe, verifies your policy, takes down what happened and gives you a claim number right away.

"I was rear-ended yesterday and need to file a claim."

What happens during a call

The voice runs on ElevenLabs; quoting and claims run on a FastAPI backend.

  1. You speakThe agent turns your speech into text and works out whether you want a quote or a claim.
  2. It asks questionsOne or two at a time, then reads your details back to confirm.
  3. It calls the backendThe API asks three carriers in parallel, or verifies your policy and saves the claim.
  4. You get an answerPrices read aloud, or a claim number, plus clear next steps.
