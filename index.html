What it does

Two calls, handled end to end

The agent talks naturally, asks one question at a time, and only acts on details you actually give it.

Get a car insurance quote

Tell it about yourself and your car. It asks every carrier in parallel and reads back prices, cheapest first.

  • Liberty Shield, Summit Mutual and Harborline quoted at once
  • Skips a carrier that's down or slow
  • Saves your choice and gives you a quote reference
Try: "I'd like a quote for full coverage on a 2019 Toyota Corolla."

Report an accident claim

It checks you're safe, verifies your policy, takes down what happened and gives you a claim number right away.

  • Safety check first, with a 911 prompt if needed
  • Verifies policy number and date of birth
  • Flags high priority if someone is hurt or the car can't be driven
Try: "I was rear-ended yesterday and need to file a claim."
How it works

From your voice to a result in seconds

The voice runs on ElevenLabs. Quoting and claims run on a FastAPI backend the agent calls mid-conversation.

1

You speak

Your speech is transcribed live and the agent works out whether you want a quote or a claim.

2

It asks questions

One at a time, then reads your details back so you can confirm them.

3

It calls the API

The backend asks three carriers in parallel, or verifies your policy and saves the claim.

4

You get an answer

Prices read aloud, or a claim number, plus clear next steps.
